# Visual Ceiling II

Goal: push Three.js/WebGPU world fidelity without Scenario or imported art assets.

Focus areas:
- terrain relief and grounded geography
- district-scale composition and depth
- surface breakup and generated decals
- localized lighting and atmospheric perspective
- denser infrastructure and skyline hierarchy
- restrained bike airtime presentation
- GPU-conscious instancing and distance-aware detail

This pass remains additive and reversible so it can be compared directly against Visual Ceiling I.
